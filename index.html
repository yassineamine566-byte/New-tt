Hansswag.QR — Free & private

Hansswag.QR is a free, open‑source QR code generator built with privacy in mind. No data is stored on our servers — everything runs locally in your browser.

Generate QR codes for URLs, text, emails, phone numbers, SMS, Wi-Fi networks, and vCards. Download as PNG or SVG. All features are completely free.

Built with ❤️ using vanilla JS, QRCode.js, and glassmorphism.
